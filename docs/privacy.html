LinkedIn Job Scanner — Privacy policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

LinkedIn Job Scanner compares the text of a PDF resume you choose with job postings displayed on LinkedIn. Processing takes place on your device. The extension does not operate a server, use analytics, display advertising, or send your resume or job data to its developer or an AI service.

Information the extension handles

  • Resume: the filename and text extracted from a PDF you select. This text may contain your name, contact details, education, and employment history. The original PDF is read to extract text; the extension does not retain the PDF file.
  • Job postings: the job title, company, location, description, and URL read from open LinkedIn jobs pages. The side panel displays analysis for the active job tab.

Use and storage

Resume text is used to calculate keyword overlap with a job description. Job text is used to detect mentions of clearance, sponsorship, experience, and preferred qualifications and to locate matching text on the page.

The filename and extracted resume text are stored in this browser profile using Chrome's local extension storage. They are not synchronized through Chrome Sync. Job data and analysis are held in memory unless you enable automatic job capture in Settings.

Folder saving is off by default. Turn on Save CSV files to a folder in Settings, select a folder, grant read/write access, and save the settings. Cancelling folder selection leaves saving off. The extension creates jobs-YYYY-MM-DD.csv directly inside the selected folder, using your computer’s local date. Each day’s qualifying jobs update that day’s file, and the next day’s first save creates a new file. No Downloads API or recurring Save As dialog is used. Existing daily files are read before writing to preserve job rows. Only the selected 11 columns are retained: Apply clicked (UTC), Title, Company, Location, Job URL, Clearance, Sponsorship, Expected experience, Citizenship / work eligibility, Best resume match (%), and Best resume slot(s), including ties. Descriptions, source evidence, resume filenames, resume text, and keyword lists are excluded from CSVs. Required-clearance jobs are excluded unless inclusion is enabled.

The selected folder handle is stored in extension IndexedDB so the worker can create and update daily files while the panel is closed and after restarts, while permission remains valid. Only dated Job Scanner CSVs in that folder are read and written; the extension does not scan other files. Chrome may expire folder permission; Allow folder access in Settings requests renewed access from your click. If the folder is missing, another folder must be selected. Until access returns, jobs stay in local extension storage. Pending writes are stored locally by date. A background alarm retries about every 30 seconds while Chrome is running; Chrome may delay these alarms. Saves also retry on worker startup, qualifying job events, and restored folder access. If Excel locks a CSV, queued jobs remain local until it can be updated. Failed older dates remain queued independently when a newer file saves successfully. Turning saving off cancels scheduled retries. Turning saving off stops new captures and file writes, while keeping existing data.

The Settings popup lets you save on viewing a job or only when you click Apply or Easy Apply on LinkedIn, and set a minimum match percentage across your saved resumes. At 0% there is no match-score restriction. Sponsorship, title, location, and clearance filters can further limit saving, and you may keep the first saved version instead of updating revisits. Apply-only capture observes your click before an external company page or Easy Apply dialog opens; it does not confirm or submit an application. The observed Apply-click time may be included in the local archive and CSV. Rule changes apply to future saves and do not remove existing rows.

Sharing and limited use

The extension does not transmit, sell, or share resume text or job data with its developer or third parties. Data is used only for the resume-to-job comparison, evidence-location, and optional local job-capture features. It is not used for advertising, credit decisions, or unrelated purposes.

If you click Copy for missing skills, those skill names are written to your device's clipboard at your request.

Retention and deletion

Saved resume text remains until you click Change in the side panel or uninstall the extension. Change deletes the stored filename and text before allowing another upload. Clearing the extension's local data also removes the resume. Closing the relevant pages and panel releases their in-memory job data.

Captured postings remain in local extension storage until you use Clear saved jobs in Settings, clear extension data, or uninstall the extension. Turning capture off stops new captures and preserves saved rows. Clear saved jobs also attempts to empty today’s CSV in the selected folder. Earlier dated files remain on disk. If the write fails, the file remains unchanged and can be emptied manually. Clearing extension data or uninstalling removes the stored folder handle and archive, but the CSV and any other copies remain on your device.

Permissions and other services

Local storage keeps your selected resume text, capture settings, and captured jobs. The sidePanel permission provides the analysis panel. The alarms permission schedules retries of queued CSV writes while saving is enabled; the retry alarm is cleared when all queued writes finish or saving is disabled. No downloads permission is requested. Read/write access to the selected folder is granted through Chrome’s folder picker. LinkedIn host access identifies matching tabs; the content script runs on LinkedIn jobs pages to read job descriptions and highlight evidence. The scripting permission reconnects the bundled reader on existing LinkedIn jobs tabs when it is missing, including navigation from the home page without a page reload and tabs open during extension updates. It does not inject remote code. The extension does not request access to all websites or the browser's history database.

LinkedIn and Google Chrome operate independently and have their own privacy policies. This extension is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by LinkedIn.

Contact and changes

For questions, use the support contact shown in the extension's Chrome Web Store listing. Please do not include your resume or sensitive personal information in a public support request. This policy will be updated if the extension's data practices change.
